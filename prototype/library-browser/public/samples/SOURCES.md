## Geometric fixtures

`alpha.svg`, `lines.svg`, `tall.svg`, `wide.svg`, `tiny.svg`: self-made exact rectangles, circles and straight-line geometry, dedicated to the public domain under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They test alpha edges, fine lines, extreme aspect ratios and small images. They are not illustrations or photographs. The deliberately broken data URL is a single-file failure fixture.

The Great Wave image is a modern recut copy after the Hokusai print, as identified by the source page, rather than the original impression.
